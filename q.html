Let's Meat

Located in the heart of Calabar, our grill restaurant brings you the ultimate open-flame dining experience, serving up sizzling, perfectly seasoned meats and seafood infused with rich, local spices, From tender, slow-roasted suya and smoky grilled croaker to juicy steaks charred to perfection, every dish is crafted fresh over glowing coals to pack bold, unforgettable flavor. Paired with chilled drinks, crisp sides, and a warm, vibrant atmosphere, it’s the perfect spot to gather with friends, relax after a long day, and enjoy the finest barbecue in town.
